FAQ

Trust, limits, and launch questions.

The copy is intentionally precise so the marketing surface does not promise more than the product can actually deliver.

Is CoverWise an insurer or broker?

No. CoverWise helps people understand the insurance policy they already have. It is not a broker, and it does not replace the insurer or the policy document.

What file types can I upload?

The backend accepts policy PDFs and common image/document formats so scanned policies can be analyzed as well.

How does the app handle answers it cannot fully confirm?

It should say what it found, what it could not find, and point the user back to the source document instead of pretending certainty.

What should the user verify before relying on an answer?

Any binding or high-stakes decision should be verified with the policy document and the insurer. CoverWise is a helper, not the source of legal truth.
