Three steps to clarity

Spend less time reading 40-page policy documents alone. CoverWise extracts and organizes key points so you can review the source text yourself.

1

Upload your policy

Pick the policy document from your phone or email. Password-protected files can be supported depending on scan quality. Some parts of the workflow may work offline; policy text processing may require a network connection.

2

CoverWise reads it

The app extracts coverage, exclusions, benefits, waiting periods, and premium details — organized on one screen.

3

Ask anything

"Am I covered for maternity?" "What's my deductible?" "How do I file a claim?" Get sourced answers where the document context supports a clear response.
