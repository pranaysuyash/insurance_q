### Forbidden-copy scan (all 11 screens)

Terms searched: recommend, best, guarantee, score, premium, estimate, should, save money, cheap, will cover, is covered, eligible, suggest. No advice, recommendation, guarantee, score, or premium-estimate wording was found.

- "Premium" on the insurance card (insurance_card_screen.dart:165-169) comes from `summary.formattedPremium` (policy_summary.dart:12, 140-144), which is an extracted value, not an estimate. The label should say it is as stated in the policy so a user does not read it as a quote.
- "Should" in documents_screen.dart and documents_list.dart appears only in code, not user copy.

### Emergency contacts check (emergency_screen.dart and data/insurer_directory.dart)

- No obvious placeholder patterns were found (no 0000, XXXX, 1234, 9999, or "TBD"). This is pattern-level only.
- The directory header (insurer_directory.dart:1) says "Verified". No source URL, as-of date, verifier, or test is recorded anywhere in the file. The "Verified" label is therefore unsupported by repo evidence.
- Format anomalies to check against insurer sites: United India "1800 425 33333" (insurer_directory.dart:100, five-digit last group), ICICI Lombard "1800 2666" (46, shorter than the others), and generic mailboxes used as claims email for New India "tech.support@" (87), National "customer.relations@" (94), and Oriental "csd@" (108).
- Live verification was not possible in this audit (static scope). Treat every number and email as unverified until the owner checks each against the insurer's official site.

## 3. Items not verified (requires Tier 2 or higher)

- Insurer hotline numbers, claims emails, and website URLs (UX-B-02). Requires owner check against official sources, and ideally a manual call or email test.
- Support address domain `coverwise.app` (UX-B-14). Requires DNS and mailbox check.
- Whether restore, rename, and change-type persist archived state on the server (UX-B-07). Requires a backend round trip test.
- Whether relationship values from real documents include non-canonical strings (UX-B-11). Requires sampling real extraction output.
