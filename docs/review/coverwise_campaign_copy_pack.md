# CoverWise Campaign Copy Pack

## Core promise

**Understand your insurance policy before you need it.**

CoverWise turns dense policy documents into plain-language answers, with the source context you need to ask better questions and make informed next steps.

## Short variants

- Make your policy easier to use.
- Plain-language answers from your policy document.
- Know what to look for before you file a claim.
- Your policy, explained with context.

## Search and social variants

### Variant A: clarity

**Headline:** Stop searching through policy PDFs.  
**Body:** Upload your insurance document and get a clearer view of coverage, exclusions, evidence, and next questions. CoverWise helps you understand the document; it does not replace your insurer or policy terms.

### Variant B: preparation

**Headline:** Be ready before the claim moment.  
**Body:** Find the parts of your policy that matter when time is short. CoverWise helps you ask better questions using the document you already have.

### Variant C: trust

**Headline:** Answers should show their context.  
**Body:** CoverWise keeps explanations tied to your policy document so you can read, question, and verify what you are seeing.

## Prohibited claims

Do not use:

- “Guaranteed coverage.”
- “We will get your claim approved.”
- “Your claim is covered.”
- “Instant insurer decision.”
- “Legal or financial advice.”
- “Never pay out of pocket.”
- “We replace your insurer.”

## Safer replacements

- “Helps you understand possible coverage and exclusions in your document.”
- “Highlights questions and evidence to discuss with your insurer.”
- “Use the policy wording and your insurer’s decision as the source of truth.”
- “CoverWise is an explanation tool, not an insurer, claims adjuster, or legal adviser.”

## Email launch sequence

### Email 1: the problem

**Subject:** Your policy should be easier to use

Insurance documents are written for completeness, not clarity. CoverWise helps you find the important parts in plain language before a stressful moment arrives.

### Email 2: the workflow

**Subject:** Start with the document you already have

Upload your policy, review the structured explanation, and ask focused questions. Keep the original wording nearby and use your insurer for the final decision.

### Email 3: the trust boundary

**Subject:** A clearer question is a better next step

CoverWise does not approve claims or change your coverage. It helps you understand what your policy says, spot uncertainty, and prepare a better conversation with the insurer.

## Support response baseline

“CoverWise explains information found in the document you provide. It cannot confirm eligibility, approve a claim, interpret missing policy pages, or replace your insurer’s decision. Please check the original policy and contact the insurer for a binding answer.”
