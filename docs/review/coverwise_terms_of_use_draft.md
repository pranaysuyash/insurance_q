## The service

CoverWise helps users organize and understand information in insurance documents they provide. It is not an insurer, broker, claims adjuster, lawyer, financial adviser, or emergency service.

## No binding coverage decision

CoverWise explanations are informational. The original policy, endorsements, insurer communications, and the insurer’s decision control. CoverWise does not approve claims, confirm eligibility, guarantee payment, or change policy terms.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for providing documents they are authorized to use, checking that uploads are complete and legible, reviewing explanations against the original document, protecting their account and device, and contacting the insurer for binding answers or deadlines.

## Accuracy and limitations

Document processing and generated explanations can be incomplete or incorrect, especially when pages are missing, scans are poor, terms are ambiguous, or the document is outside the supported format. Users should not rely on CoverWise alone for urgent, legal, financial, medical, or claims decisions.

## Acceptable use

Users must not use the service unlawfully, upload information they do not have permission to process, attempt to disrupt the service, or submit malicious content.
