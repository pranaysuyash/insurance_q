## Context

After the wide-open brainstorm panel (10 roles, 33+ candidate names, synthesis in `brainstorm_2026-07-28/README.md`), the founder reviewed the outputs and the agent's recommended shortlist (Steady, Tippani, Amanat). The founder rejected the panel's directions through three sequential redirects, each sharpening the brief. The redirects are reproduced below verbatim, with the founder's text in blockquotes and the consequence of each noted.

---

## Redirect 1 — "Tippani sounds like a commentary app"

**Founder's verbatim:**

> my take, all names are poor, tippani sounds as if its a commentary app not something that would help a user understand their insurances, so think like this what alternate but simpler names exist that make the user understand - so maybe samajh is something people know so what mix can we use for insurance etc?

**Consequence:**
- Killed **Tippani** (टिप्पणी literally means "comment/remark/criticism"). The Cartographer's "annotation layer" metaphor was structurally elegant but missed the word's actual connotation — a commentary positions the product as opinion, not comprehension.
- Redirected toward comprehension-vocabulary (*samajh*) mixed with the category word (*insurance/policy/bima*).
- First response proposed PolicySamajh, BimaSamajh, SamajhDaar, SahiBima, BimaJaano, PolicySahi. Agent pick: **PolicySamajh**.

---

## Redirect 2 — "Nothing that sounds like it needs legality or regulatory"

**Founder's verbatim:**

> but i dont want to get into something that sounds it needs legality backing or regulatory etc

**Consequence:**
- Killed the **entire comprehension + category family** proposed in response to Redirect 1: PolicySamajh, BimaSamajh, SahiBima, Insurance Khata, BimaBol, Policy Reader, SamajhDaar-as-evaluator.
- Structural reason (agent analysis, which the founder's instinct confirmed): pairing "understand/know/right/correct" with "insurance/policy/bima" reads as **advisory** — "this app tells you whether your insurance is right." Advisory positioning in a regulated category is the exact boundary the product refuses. The product reads and cites; it does not assess or recommend.
- This invalidated the direction that 4 panel roles (Operator, Cartographer, Outsider, Archivist) had independently championed. They optimized for *warm Hindi household trust* and forgot the regulatory fence.

---

## Redirect 3 — "Generic RAG app, not India-only, not insurance-only"

**Founder's verbatim:**

> and why only hindi , its not like we would stay local to india only, its a generic app , functionality is rag , just that it targets the insurance

**Consequence:**
- Killed **all Indic-rooted names** — Amanat, Bahi, Kosha, Prama, Apta, Lekh, Sambhal, Samjho, Hisaab, Sahaj, Sahi, BimaBol, BimaSamajh, PolicySamajh. The entire School B (vernacular-functional) direction.
- Corrected the product definition the panel had been working against: the app is a **generic RAG-over-documents tool** that *launches* on insurance, not an India-specific insurance product. A Hindi/Urdu/Sanskrit name is a foreign object in Kenya, Indonesia, Brazil, or a US ex-pat market.
- Collapsed both panel schools (A and B) — both were anchored to India and to insurance; the real product is neither.

## The corrected brief (what emerged from the three redirects)

The corrected brief is captured in full in `decision_process_record_2026-07-28.md` §3. In summary, the name must be:

1. **Category-agnostic** — no insurance/finance/protection/domain lock-in.
2. **Globally portable** — English-neutral, no language lock-in.
3. **Zero advisory/regulatory signal** — names the function (read/ask/answer/cite/source), not a judgment.
4. **Survives crisis-state retrievability** — the panel's binding operational test.
5. **Ownable** — coined or distinctive enough to register in Classes 9/36/42 across jurisdictions.

The corrected candidate set (generated post-redirect): **Sourced, Pageback, Cited** (with Readr, SourceIt, AskDoc, Grounded, Verbatim as alternates considered and narrowed out). Full reasoning in `decision_process_record_2026-07-28.md` §4.

## Redirect 4 — "App-specific now, portfolio-generic later" (the layering reframe)

**Founder's verbatim:**

> but asksource is generic while we are actively only doing ins. mayeb later if i make more rag apps it goes under asksource, for now it maybe insrag (insurance + rag), insq etc as well?

**Consequence:**
- Killed the **single-name-is-both-app-and-portfolio** assumption that Redirects 1–3 had implicitly created. The founder re-asserted the **layering** originally documented in `rename_strategy_and_inventory_2026-07-28.md` §1 (company / portfolio / app layers) but lost during the panel.
- The corrected brief splits into two layers, decided at two different times:
  - **App layer (decide now):** insurance-specific, because that is what the app *does today*.
  - **Portfolio/umbrella layer (decide later):** generic RAG brand (AskSource or similar), decided when product #2 is real.
- This vindicates the Executioner's kill-test condition #4 ("Drop the portfolio constraint for v1. Name the app"). The panel and the agent had re-collapsed the layers; the founder re-separated them.

**Founder's proposed candidates (insurance-specific):**
- **InsRAG** (insurance + rag)
- **InsQ** (insurance + question)

**Agent analysis of founder's proposals:**
- **InsRAG — flagged for a real negative-load problem.** "RAG" is developer jargon (target users have never heard it). Worse, in Indian English "ragging" specifically means college hazing/bullying — a strongly-negative, well-known term. "InsRag" risks reading as "insurance ragging" = the app that bullies/criticizes your insurance. That is the same commentary-app problem Redirect 1 rejected (Tippani), resurfacing in a different costume. Agent recommendation: kill InsRAG on the negative-load basis.
- **InsQ — workable but ticker-leaning.** Short, punchy, "Q" = Q&A. But reads like a stock ticker or pharma molecule (compare actual tickers INSW, INSM). Phonetically "ins-kyoo" — could be heard as "risky" in some accents. "Ins" standalone is slightly ambiguous outside finance contexts (insert? inspections? insulin?). Workable if a crisp technical identity is wanted; less so if warmth is wanted.

**Agent's proposed candidates in the same direction (insurance + function, no advisory signal, no jargon):**
- **InsAsk** — ask questions about your insurance. Clean; functional not advisory; 2 syllables; ownable compound; "ask" is warm. Agent's pick in this direction.
- **PolicyQ** — policy questions. "Policy" is the universal loanword (crosses Tamil/Telugu/Bengali better than "ins"); ownable.
- **PolicyAsk** — ask about your policy. Longer but clearest; no abbreviation ambiguity.

**The naming principle the founder's redirect reaffirmed:** name the *function* (ask/answer/question) + the *domain* (insurance/policy), without naming *judgment* (right/correct/understand) — because judgment = advisory = regulatory risk.

**Status of the corrected candidate set:**
- **App layer (insurance-specific):** InsAsk, PolicyQ, InsQ, PolicyAsk (InsRAG killed on negative-load).
- **Portfolio layer (generic, deferred):** AskSource (or similar) — decided when product #2 is real.

Full reasoning and the layering decision in `decision_process_record_2026-07-28.md` §3–4 (updated).

## Why this transcript matters

The three redirects did more to sharpen the answer than all 10 panel roles combined. The panel generated the option space; the founder's rejections cut it. The cuts are the decision logic — without this transcript, a future reader would see the panel's Indic-rooted shortlist (Tippani, Amanat, Kosha) and conclude the project landed there, when in fact those directions were all killed by the founder's three sentences.

The redirects also surfaced the corrected product definition — **"a category-agnostic RAG product that launches on insurance"** — which is arguably more valuable than any name and should inform future product, positioning, and architecture decisions.
