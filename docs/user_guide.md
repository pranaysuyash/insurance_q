## Introduction

The Insurance Policy Parser & QA App is designed to help you better understand and manage your insurance policies. The app allows you to:

- Upload and organize your insurance policy documents
- Extract and view key policy information in an easy-to-understand format
- Ask natural language questions about your coverage and get accurate answers
- Compare different policies or versions side-by-side
- Set up alerts for important policy dates and events
- Securely store and access your policy information

Whether you have health, auto, home, life, or other insurance types, our app can help you make sense of complex policy language and quickly find the information you need.

## Uploading Insurance Policies

### Supported Document Types

The system currently supports:
- PDF documents (text-based or scanned)
- Standard insurance policy formats from major providers
- Documents up to 50MB in size

### Upload Process

1. From your dashboard, click the "Upload Policy" button
2. Select a PDF file from your device or drag and drop it into the upload area
3. Provide basic information about the policy:
   - Insurance type (Health, Auto, Home, Life, etc.)
   - Insurance provider
   - Policy nickname (optional)
4. Click "Upload" to begin the processing

### Processing Status

After uploading, the system will process your document:

1. **Initial Processing**: Basic document verification and preparation
2. **Text Extraction**: Converting your policy to machine-readable text
3. **Information Extraction**: Identifying key policy details
4. **Indexing**: Making your policy searchable for questions

This process typically takes 1-3 minutes depending on document size and complexity. You'll see a progress indicator during processing.

### Common Upload Issues

- **File Format Error**: Ensure your document is a PDF
- **File Size Error**: Try reducing the file size if it exceeds 50MB
- **Poor Quality Scan**: For best results, upload clearly scanned documents
- **Processing Error**: If processing fails, try uploading a different version of the document

## Policy Dashboard

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of all your policies with key information:

- Policy cards organized by type
- Status indicators for active/expired policies
- Quick access to policy details
- Recent activity summary
- Upcoming important dates

### Policy Cards

Each policy card shows:
- Policy type icon
- Insurer name
- Policy number
- Effective dates
- Premium amount
- Status indicator

Click on any policy card to view detailed information.

### Policy Detail View

The detail view shows comprehensive information extracted from your policy:

- **Summary Tab**: Overview of key policy details
- **Coverage Tab**: Detailed coverage information
- **Exclusions Tab**: What's not covered by your policy
- **Documents Tab**: Access to original and processed documents
- **Timeline Tab**: Important dates and policy changes
- **Activity Tab**: History of interactions with this policy

### Editing Policy Information

If the system didn't correctly extract some information:

1. Navigate to the relevant section
2. Click the "Edit" button next to the information
3. Enter the correct information
4. Click "Save" to update

The system will retain your corrections for future reference.

## Asking Questions About Your Policies

### Question Interface

The natural language question interface allows you to ask about your policies in everyday language:

1. Click the "Ask a Question" button on the dashboard or policy detail page
2. Type your question in the text field
3. Optionally select specific policies to search, or search across all policies
4. Click "Submit" to get your answer

### Example Questions

You can ask a wide variety of questions, such as:

- "What is my deductible for hospital stays?"
- "Does my policy cover physical therapy visits?"
- "When is my premium payment due?"
- "How much coverage do I have for prescription medications?"
- "What are the exclusions for my home insurance policy?"
- "Is maternity care covered under my plan?"
- "What happens if I miss a payment?"
- "How do I file a claim for dental work?"

### Understanding Answers

Answers will include:
- Direct response to your question
- Source references from your policy documents
- Confidence indication (how certain the system is)
- Relevant policy sections or page numbers
- Explanations of insurance terms when needed
- Related information that might be helpful

### Follow-up Questions

You can ask follow-up questions and the system will maintain context from your conversation:

1. After receiving an answer, simply type your follow-up question
2. The system will remember the context of your previous questions
3. You can continue the conversation naturally

### Saving Important Questions

To save important questions and answers for future reference:

1. Click the "Save" icon next to any answer
2. Add an optional note or tag
3. Access saved questions later from the "Saved Q&A" section

## Policy Comparison

### Starting a Comparison

To compare two or more policies:

1. Go to the "Compare" section from the main menu
2. Select the policies you want to compare (2-3 recommended)
3. Click "Compare Selected" to generate the comparison

### Comparison Types

The system supports different comparison types:

- **Same Policy Type**: Compare similar policies (e.g., two health insurance policies)
- **Same Policy Versions**: Compare different versions of the same policy
- **Different Policy Types**: Compare coverage across different insurance types

### Comparison Views

The comparison will display:

- **Side-by-Side View**: Direct comparison of key policy elements
- **Summary View**: Overview of main differences and similarities
- **Detailed View**: In-depth comparison of specific coverage areas

### Analyzing Differences

The comparison highlights:
- Coverage differences (highlighted in color)
- Premium and deductible variations
- Exclusion differences
- Coverage limits and maximums
- Network differences (for health insurance)

### Exporting Comparisons

To save or share your comparison:

1. Click the "Export" button
2. Choose your preferred format (PDF, CSV, or print view)
3. Save to your device or share via email

## Alerts and Notifications

### Available Alert Types

The system can alert you about:

- **Renewal Dates**: When policies are approaching expiration
- **Payment Due Dates**: Reminders for premium payments
- **Coverage Changes**: Notification when policy terms change
- **Document Updates**: When new policy versions are detected
- **Custom Alerts**: User-defined alerts for specific dates or events

### Setting Up Alerts

To configure alerts:

1. Go to the "Alerts" section from the main menu
2. Select the alert types you want to receive
3. Configure timing preferences (how far in advance)
4. Choose notification methods (email, in-app, or both)
5. Save your preferences

### Managing Notifications

You can manage how you receive notifications:

1. Go to "Settings" > "Notifications"
2. Toggle notification types on/off
3. Set quiet hours for notifications
4. Update email preferences

### Snoozing or Dismissing Alerts

When you receive an alert:
- Click "Snooze" to be reminded later
- Click "Dismiss" to clear the alert
- Click "View Details" to see related policy information

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Issue**: Document upload fails
- **Solution**: Check file format and size, ensure it's a valid PDF under 50MB

**Issue**: Policy information extraction is incomplete
- **Solution**: Some complex policies may require manual editing; use the edit function to add missing details

**Issue**: Question answering is slow
- **Solution**: Complex questions across multiple policies may take longer to process; try more specific questions

**Issue**: Can't find specific policy information
- **Solution**: Use the search function or ask a direct question about the specific information

**Issue**: Session timed out
- **Solution**: Log back in; your data is saved securely

### Getting Support

If you encounter problems:

1. Check the FAQ section in the Help menu
2. Click "Contact Support" in the Help menu
3. Provide details about your issue
4. Include screenshots if possible

Support hours: Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST

## Privacy and Security

### Data Protection

Your policy documents and personal information are protected:

- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Documents are stored in secure cloud storage
- Personal information is separated from policy data
- Access controls restrict who can view your information

### Privacy Controls

You can control your privacy settings:

1. Go to "Settings" > "Privacy"
2. Review what data is stored
3. Configure data retention preferences
4. Manage third-party integrations

### Security Best Practices

For maximum security:

- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Log out when using shared devices
- Regularly review account activity
- Update your email and contact information
- Don't share your account credentials

### Data Deletion

To remove your data:

1. Go to "Settings" > "Data"
2. Select "Delete My Account"
3. Confirm your decision
4. Your data will be permanently removed within 30 days
